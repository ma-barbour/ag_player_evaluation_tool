Note Re: Expected Goals

Here are a couple of important things to know about the 18 Skaters xG data:

  1. Only a shot-on-goal receives an xG value from the model (other shot attempts are ignored, which is not typical of other xG models).
  2. A shot on an empty net (i.e., an empty net goal) does not receive an xG value from the model.
  3. The xG values shown here are "adjusted" to reflect shooting talent (a skater with a history of scoring above "expected" gets higher xG values and vice versa).
